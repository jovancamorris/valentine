I think I finally understand that not every love is meant to be owned. Some are only meant to be felt deeply, then remembered beautifully.

And strangely, instead of destroying me, it became the reason I started moving forward again.

Maybe you were never meant to stay in my life forever. Maybe you were meant to become the most beautiful memory, I’ll always carry with me — the person who taught me how sincere love could feel.

A part of me will probably always wish it could have been us. But another part of me is finally learning to let go peacefully.

Thank you for being my favorite “what if.”

And if someday I finally find someone who truly belongs to me, a small part of my heart will still remember you as one of the most beautiful chapters of my life
